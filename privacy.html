Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

In short: EDM has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your download list, settings and files stay on your phone. Information only leaves your device when EDM connects to a link you give it, or when you use a download code.

This policy explains how EDM – Easy Download Manager (package com.easydownloadmanager.edm), developed by YOUR NAME ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the app.

Information we don't collect

We don't ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any account details. EDM contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and we don't sell or rent any information.

Information stored on your device

To work, EDM keeps the following in its private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it isn't sent to us.

Your download list: links, file names, sizes and statusTo show your downloads and continue them laterUntil you remove the download, or after the 200 most recent

Finished files are saved to your phone's Downloads folder, where they stay until you delete them.

Information that leaves your device

Websites you download from

When you add a link, EDM connects directly to that website to check the file name and size and to download the file. Like any browser, this shares your IP address, a browser identifier (user agent, which you can change in Settings) and any cookies that website has set. We never see this traffic. How the website uses it is covered by that website's own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to download files and to pause or resume when your connection changes.
  • Notifications (optional): to show download progress and tell you when a file is ready. Downloads still work if you say no.
  • Foreground service: to keep downloads running while you use other apps.

EDM saves files through Android's Downloads folder and doesn't need access to your other files, photos or media.

Security

EDM only connects to secure (https) links. Data stored by the app is kept in its private storage on your phone.

Your choices

  • Remove any download from the list, and optionally delete its file, from the download's details.
  • Turn off cookie saving, clear saved cookies, or change the user agent in Settings.
  • Choose how long unfinished downloads keep their saved progress, or never clear it.
  • Uninstall EDM to remove all of its data from your phone. Files already in your Downloads folder stay until you delete them.
